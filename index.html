How it works

  1. Create an account

    Tap Register at the top. Your name, mobile number and a password are enough.

  2. Choose a game and tournament

    Pick BGMI or Free Fire, then Solo, Duo or Squad, and an online or offline tournament.

  3. Book a slot and pay

    Fill in your team details. You get a UPI QR after booking, and your slot is confirmed once payment is verified.

  4. Play and win

    Online matches: you and your opponent see each other's UID in My bookings, create a custom room and play. Send the result and the points table updates.

Read before you play

Rules

Fair play

Hacks, scripts or teaming lead to a team ban and a cancelled prize. The admin's decision is final.

Custom rooms

You create the custom room yourself and add your opponent by the UID shown in My bookings. Start the match at the time on the tournament card.

Correct UID

Play only against the UID shown in My bookings, using the UID you registered. A different account means a cancelled result.

Refund

If a tournament is cancelled, or the slots fill up before your payment is verified, the entry fee is refunded in full.

Disconnects

If your internet drops mid-match, the match is not replayed. Make sure your connection is stable beforehand.

Offline LAN

Bring an ID proof and your team UIDs to the venue. Device details are listed on the tournament card.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

How much is the entry fee?

Solo ₹100, Duo ₹150, Squad ₹250 (per team). The fee is shown on each tournament card and is paid via UPI QR.

When do I get my prize?

After the result is verified, your prize is sent to your UPI ID within 24 hours.

How do I play my match?

After your payment is confirmed and your opponent has paid, their UID appears in My bookings. Add them in the game, create a custom room, play, then send the result screenshot.

What if the slots are full?

The button is disabled. Choose another tournament or wait for the next announcement.

What should I bring to an offline tournament?

An ID proof, your registration screenshot and your game account. Other details are on the tournament card.

What if someone cheats?

Send a screenshot or recording to our help email after the match. After review, the team may be banned.
